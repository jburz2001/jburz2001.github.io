I attended the [2026 Cal-Bridge Fall Conference](https://calbridge.org/portfolio-item/fall-conference/) at UC Irvine from 09/11--09/13.
\\
This conference provided professional development training and networking opportunities with faculty across California's public university system. Cal-Bridge was awarded $10.2 million for 2026-2027 to continue their work in helping underrepresented youth in STEM join the professoriate and research workforce.
